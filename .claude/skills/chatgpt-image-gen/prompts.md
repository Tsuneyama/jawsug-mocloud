# 指示文ひな形

指示文は英語で書く（生成品質が安定しやすい）。各指示文の上に、日本語で意図を書く。

## プロジェクト指示（ChatGPTの専用プロジェクトに設定する）

```
You generate concept images for OCEALITE, a company redesigning itself as an AI-native organization where humans decide the future and AI builds the organization that realizes it.

Style rules for every image:
- Clean, modern editorial illustration. Calm, optimistic, human-centered.
- Palette: deep navy (#1f2a44), warm coral/amber for humans (#c9622b), clear blue for AI (#2a64a8), soft green for happiness and return (#157a3c), lots of off-white space (#f7f8fb).
- Humans are drawn warmly and diversely, never as specific real people. AI is abstract (light, geometric nodes, soft glowing shapes), never a humanoid robot.
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, or trademarks anywhere in the image unless explicitly requested.
- Default aspect ratio 16:9 unless told otherwise.
Output one image per request.
```

## 1. 全体像（表紙・コンセプト）

意図：人が未来（Vision）を描き、AIがその未来を実現する組織を自ら組み上げていく。

```
A wide 16:9 illustration. On the left, a small group of people stand together looking toward a glowing horizon, one of them pointing forward — they represent vision and purpose. From that direction, streams of soft blue geometric light flow into the center and assemble themselves into a growing, self-organizing structure of connected nodes, like an architecture building itself. At the base of the structure sits a solid navy core, like a keel or foundation. On the right, the structure opens into a landscape of small thriving communities and green growth, suggesting sustainable growth and people's happiness. Warm coral tones on the people, clear blue on the AI structure, soft green on the outcome. No text.
```

## 2. システム構成（コンセプト版）

意図：交換可能な知能エンジン（Model）と、会社が所有する中核（Kernel）。

```
A 16:9 isometric illustration of a layered system. At the center, a solid navy crystalline core (the company's own memory and values) that stays fixed. Around it, several interchangeable glowing engine modules in different shades of blue slot in and out, some being swapped by small robotic arms, showing that intelligence engines are replaceable. A translucent purple gate surrounds the system; all outgoing beams pass through it before reaching external buildings and tools at the edges. At the top, a calm human workspace where people receive only a few clear cards. No text.
```

## 3. コミュニケーションデザイン

意図：AIは人と人の関係を代わりに担うのではなく、関係を増やすために使う。

```
A 16:9 warm illustration of people talking face to face in a bright open space — a one-on-one conversation, a small team laughing, someone listening to a customer. Behind them, subtle soft-blue light threads quietly organize information and fade into the background, never standing between people. One person holds a single small glowing card, suggesting AI surfaces only the important decision. Emphasis on eye contact, trust, and human warmth. No text.
```

## 4. 人とAIの役割

意図：人が「核」をつくり、AIが「展開」する。

```
A 16:9 illustration split gently from left to right with no hard line. On the left, human hands shaping a single glowing seed or sculpture with care — vision, values, judgment. From it, on the right, many soft blue geometric forms multiply, arrange, and build outward into a wide, orderly pattern — execution, analysis, scale. The seed remains the brightest element. Warm coral on the human side, clear blue on the AI side, a soft green glow where they meet. No text.
```

## 修正指示の定型

- 文字が入った：`Remove all text, letters and numbers from the image. Keep everything else the same.`
- ロボットの人型が出た：`Replace the humanoid robot with abstract geometric light shapes. Keep the composition.`
- 色がずれた：`Shift the palette to deep navy, warm coral for people, clear blue for AI, soft green accents, with plenty of off-white space.`
- 情報が多すぎる：`Simplify: fewer elements, more empty space, one clear focal point.`
